A note to everyone who finds this page

We set out to build a doorway to the new economy. This is the story of why we never opened it.

iCryptonia is no longer active. We wanted to leave something better than an empty page here: an honest account of what we tried, what stopped us, and what we took away.

Read the story ↓
Chapter one · The idea

Crypto deserved the discipline of real fund management.

In 2018, crypto assets were everywhere in the headlines and almost nowhere in a sensible portfolio. Most people who wanted exposure had two options: trade on their own with little knowledge, or stay out entirely.

We believed there should be a third way. Three of us founded iCryptonia on a simple conviction: the principles that have served investors for decades, such as diversification, risk matching, transparent fees and professional management, could be applied to this new asset class.

Our promise was “Your Doorway to Investing in the New Economy.” We meant it as a doorway that ordinary people could walk through safely.

Chapter two · What we built

A simple platform for people without the time or expertise.

We designed managed crypto portfolios and funds for individual investors, each built around a person’s goals and appetite for risk. These were the principles behind the product:

Low minimum

Start with $500, not a private-banking balance.

Easy platform

A fast, simple way to begin investing, with no exchange accounts or wallets to wrestle with.

Affordable, transparent fees

A clear fee structure with no hidden costs.

Security first

A secure and trusted way to hold and manage crypto assets.

We built the product, the portfolios and the platform. What we could not build on our own was the ground it needed to stand on.

Chapter three · What stood in the way

Three walls, all at once.

Managing other people’s money is, rightly, one of the most regulated activities there is. When we tried to bring iCryptonia to market, we found that the rules, partners and infrastructure it needed did not exist yet.

  • 01
    No licensing pathwayNo regulator had a framework under which a managed crypto portfolio or fund could be licensed. We had no door to knock on.
  • 02
    No banking or custodyBanks were not prepared to serve a crypto-asset business, and regulated, institutional-grade custody was not available to us.
  • 03
    No route to retail investorsOffering crypto investment products to everyday investors, the very people we built iCryptonia for, was not permitted.

Any one of these could have been worked around in time. Together, they meant there was no responsible way to launch. We would not ask people to trust us with their savings before the protections they deserved were in place.

So we made the hardest decision a founding team can make. We stopped.

Chapter four · What we learned

The experiment ended. The education didn’t.

iCryptonia never served a single investor, but it taught its founders more than many companies that did. These are the lessons we carry with us.

In finance, regulation comes first.

We started with the product and expected the license to follow. It works the other way around. In regulated finance the license is not paperwork around the product; it is part of the product. The conversation with the regulator should be the first one, not the last.

Being right too early looks a lot like being wrong.

The idea of professionally managed, diversified crypto portfolios was sound, and the world has since moved toward it. But timing is not a detail. A good idea that arrives before its market and its rules still cannot launch.

Infrastructure matters more than the interface.

An app is the easy part. Custody, banking relationships, compliance and settlement rails are what make a financial product real. Without them, even a beautiful platform has nowhere to stand.

Trust has to be earned before it can be invested.

Retail investors did not need another product. They needed understanding and confidence first. Education and trust are not marketing extras; they are the foundation that every new asset class has to be built on.

Epilogue

Thank you.

To everyone who believed in the idea, gave us advice, opened a door, or told us honestly that a door was closed: thank you. You were part of this story.

The doorway is closed. The lessons stay with us.
